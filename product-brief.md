# Thyme Machine MVP brief

## Positioning

Lebanese at heart, open to anyone who connects with the food, humor, music, and warmth.

## Why people join

Dating starts with a shared cultural signal instead of a blank profile. The product gives people an easy first conversation and a more memorable first date at Thyme Machine.

## Original matching loop

Each person answers playful prompts called **Thyme or Time?**. The system creates a Taste Map from:

* Zaatar intensity
* Olive oil confidence
* Manouche order
* Lebanese culture and language connection
* Humor and conversation energy
* Ideal first date pace

The app shows a limited daily set of “Table Matches,” each with a short explanation of the connection. Users can send one expressive reaction before matching, such as “Extra zaatar,” “Pass the olive oil,” or “Let’s make time.”

## Fast, human onboarding

Signup should take under a minute. Offer email, Facebook, and Instagram entry points, but import photos only after clear consent and let the user choose exactly what appears. Ask one taste question and one intent question before showing the first match. Never silently scrape contacts, publish imported photos, or infer sensitive identity traits.

## Conversation design

Every match arrives with one specific, playful opener tied to the shared Taste Map. Examples include “What is the most controversial thing you put on a manouche?” and “Are you early to the table or fashionably Lebanese?” The opener can be sent with one tap, edited, or ignored.

## Monetization

* Free: profile, Taste Map, limited daily Table Matches, matching, and chat
* Thyme Plus: more daily discovery, advanced Taste Map filters, rewind, and see who liked you
* Thyme Select: curated introductions, priority visibility, and access to special Thyme Machine events
* Optional one time boosts, used sparingly

Subscriptions must use Apple and Google compliant in app billing, with clear pricing, renewal, cancellation, privacy, and refund information.

## Release boundary

The first prototype is private and uses mock profiles only. No store submission, paid product, external messaging, or production user data is enabled.
